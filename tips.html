The whole shelf

Every tip in the toolbox.

E-bikes
Your e-bike works twice as hard
An e-bike carries more weight and moves faster, so braking is harsher and every part takes more strain. Nuts and bolts are constantly fighting to stay tight, and pads, chains and tires wear out two to three times faster than on a regular bike. Get it checked every few months, not once a year - on an e-bike, maintenance is a safety thing, not just a performance thing.
Tires
Check your pressure before every ride
The right number is printed right on the tire's sidewall. Low tires are the number-one cause of flats and make you work twice as hard for the same speed. A thirty-second squeeze-and-pump saves you a tube and a tired ride.
Drivetrain
A dry chain is a slow chain
If you hear squeaking, your chain is already crying for help. Wipe it down, add a few drops of lube, then wipe off the excess - or you'll turn your chain into a grit magnet that wears out fast.
Brakes
Don't wait for metal on metal
If braking sounds like a screech or feels spongy, your pads are talking to you. Pads are cheap; rotors and rims are not. Catch it early and save yourself the bigger bill.
Care
Salt air is a drivetrain's worst enemy
Living this close to the water is a gift, but salt and sand eat metal. Keep your bike out of the weather when you can, and give the chain a quick wipe after a beach ride - it'll last years longer.
Safety
The 30-second ABC check
Before every ride: Air (tires firm), Brakes (both grab), Chain (spins clean and quiet). Do those three and you'll catch most problems before they ever leave the driveway.
Drivetrain
More lube isn't better
A dripping, greasy chain collects sand and grit and actually wears out faster. A little lube, wiped clean, beats a lot every single time.
Kids
Kids' bikes need love too
They get dropped, left in the rain and grown into. A quick tune keeps the brakes grabbing and the wheels true - and keeps your kid safe on every ride.
Safety
Give it a wiggle
Grab the seat, the handlebars and each wheel and give them a firm wiggle. Anything that moves is a bolt that needs attention - and on e-bikes, vibration works bolts loose faster than you'd think.
Tune-ups
Ride it, don't ignore it
If it's been about a year, or your bike is shifting rough, braking soft, or making a new noise, it's time. Small fixes now almost always beat the big repair bill later.
