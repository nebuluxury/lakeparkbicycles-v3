Otium 2 Cruiser

The Otium 2 steps everything up: a bigger 768Wh battery for up to 60 miles, a full aluminum ultra-low step-through frame, and a crisp 2.4-inch color display. It is the premium cruiser for riders who want extra range and a plush, upright ride around town.
