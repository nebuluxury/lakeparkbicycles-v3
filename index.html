What we do

Two things, done right.

The $129 tune-up

Our most-popular Full Tune-Up is $129 this summer (regularly $169), delivered free - and the quick Safety Check is still $99. We come to you, tune it on our bench, and bring it back within 72 hours, 100% happy or we fix it free. Pedal bikes, any make and any age; e-bikes handled separately.

Rentals, delivered

Men's, women's and kids' bikes plus e-bikes, delivered to your hotel, condo or home with helmet and lock included. Free delivery on rentals of 3 days or more; otherwise a flat $25, or pick up at the shop free.

Repairs, any make

Fifty years of wrench time on every brand - including e-bike motors and batteries. Flats, brakes, gears, full overhauls. Clear prices, free basic adjustments, always.
