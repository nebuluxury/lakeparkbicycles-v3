Why Retrospec

Good bikes that don't cost a fortune.

Retrospec has been building good-looking, dependable bikes at down-to-earth prices since 2008. Clean design, solid parts, and a bike for every age and ability - from the bike-curious to the weekend adventurer. It's a line we're happy to put our name behind.

We picked them because they fit how this shop has always worked: get a good bike under someone at a fair price, set it up right, and stand behind it. Every new bike leaves here fully assembled, tuned and safety-checked by Tony - not flat-packed in a box.
