Lake Park questions

Good to know.

Do you really come all the way to Lake Park?

Yes - Lake Park is well within our service area, just up the road - we're practically neighbors. Free doorstep pickup and delivery on the Full Tune-Up and Signature Overhaul; a flat $25 on the $99 Safety Check.

Can you deliver rental bikes to my Lake Park hotel or rental?

Absolutely. Cruisers, e-bikes and kids' bikes delivered to your door anywhere in Lake Park, helmet and lock included. Delivery is free on rentals of three days or more.

Do you work on bikes you did not sell?

Always - any make, any model, any age. Department-store bike or boutique build, it gets the same bench and the same care.

Do you service e-bikes in Lake Park?

Yes, but separately: e-bike diagnostic and service starts at $79 because motors, batteries and firmware take different tools and time.
