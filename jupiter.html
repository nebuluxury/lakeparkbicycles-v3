Where to ride in Jupiter

Our favorite Jupiter rides.

Jupiter is one of the best places to ride in the county - shady river trails, a breezy inlet, and quiet beach paths. Here are four we send folks to.

Ride
Riverbend Park
Ten miles of hard-packed shell trails winding along the Loxahatchee through shady oaks and marsh - the best family ride in Jupiter.
Ride
Jupiter Beach & the Lighthouse
A flat, breezy spin past the historic Jupiter Inlet Lighthouse and down along the sand.
Ride
Ocean Cay Park
Quiet paved paths and a boardwalk out to the dune line - gentle enough for the littlest riders.
Ride
Jupiter Ridge Natural Area
Sandy trails and boardwalks through coastal scrub with surprise ocean overlooks - a quieter, wilder ride.

Jupiter questions

Good to know.

Do you really come all the way to Jupiter?

Yes - Jupiter is well within our service area, about 15 minutes up US-1. Free doorstep pickup and delivery on the Full Tune-Up and Signature Overhaul; a flat $25 on the $99 Safety Check.

Can you deliver rental bikes to my Jupiter hotel or rental?

Absolutely. Cruisers, e-bikes and kids' bikes delivered to your door anywhere in Jupiter, helmet and lock included. Delivery is free on rentals of three days or more.

Do you work on bikes you did not sell?

Always - any make, any model, any age. Department-store bike or boutique build, it gets the same bench and the same care.

Do you service e-bikes in Jupiter?

Yes, but separately: e-bike diagnostic and service starts at $79 because motors, batteries and firmware take different tools and time.
